  • 1. Speed: v = d / t
  • 2. Distance: d = v × t
  • 3. Newton's 2nd Law: F = ma
  • 4. Gravitational Force: F = G (m₁m₂ / r²)
  • 5. Orbital Velocity: v = √(GM / r)
  • 6. Escape Velocity: v_e = √(2GM / r)
  • 7. Fuel Usage: Total - Remaining
  • 8. Kinetic Energy: KE = ½ mv²
  • 9. Work Done: W = F × d
  • 10. Time Period: T = 2π √(r³ / GM)
